oobb_cylinder_d_shaft

OOBB Geometry Primitives: D-Shaft Cylinder

Cylinder with a flat cut from the +Y side. cut_d is the material removed in mm; the remaining flat-to-back size is 2 * radius - cut_d.

Category: OOBB Geometry Primitives
Returns: List of mode-specific rotation objects containing a cylinder minus a flat cut.
Aliases: None

Sample Notes

cut_d is a distance removed in millimetres, not a fraction or the remaining width. The flat faces +Y before rotation; zz defaults to center. Regenerate previews with python -c "from components.oobb_cylinder_d_shaft.working import test; test()".

Usage

import oobb

shaft = oobb.oobb_easy(
    shape="oobb_cylinder_d_shaft",
    type="p",
    radius=5,
    depth=12,
    cut_d=2,
    pos=[0, 0, 0],
    zz="center",
)

This starts with a 10 mm diameter cylinder and removes 2 mm from its +Y edge. The flat lies at local y=3 mm, leaving 8 mm from the flat to the opposite edge. The cylinder spans z=-6 to z=6 mm. cut_d stays in millimetres when the radius changes.

For a D-shaped hole, use type="n" and combine the returned geometry with a positive body. The cylinder and its flat are grouped before subtraction, so the flat cutter does not cut into surrounding parts. Use rot=[0,0,90] to turn the flat toward -X, or rot=[90,0,0] to put the shaft axis along -Y.

Named radii resolve separately in each render mode. For example:

shaft = oobb.oobb_easy(
    shape="oobb_cylinder_d_shaft",
    radius_name="hole_radius_m6",
    depth=12,
    cut_d=0.5,
    zz="bottom",
    mode="3dpr",
)

Limits

Use a positive constant radius and depth. cut_d=0 produces an uncut cylinder; cut_d=radius produces a half cylinder. Cuts deeper than the radius are allowed, but cut_d must remain less than the full diameter in every requested mode. Negative cuts, non-finite dimensions, and tapered cylinders are rejected.
